# Governance

CanWeSynth begins as a maintainer-led project and is designed to move toward a
small, accountable maintainer council.

## Roles

- **Contributor:** anyone with a merged change or other documented project
  contribution.
- **Reviewer:** a contributor trusted to review a defined area.
- **Maintainer:** a reviewer trusted with releases, security triage, and project
  direction.

## Becoming a reviewer or maintainer

Existing maintainers nominate people based on sustained constructive
contributions, sound technical judgment, reliable reviews, and community
conduct. Access is a responsibility, not a reward, and can be stepped down
voluntarily or removed for inactivity or conduct problems.

## Decisions

Routine decisions happen in pull requests. Cross-cutting or irreversible
changes use a public proposal issue and seek rough consensus. If consensus is
not possible, maintainers document the decision, tradeoffs, and dissent.

The instrument format, real-time safety rules, licensing, and user trust
boundaries require explicit maintainer review.
